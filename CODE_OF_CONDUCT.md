# Code of conduct

varatlas follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be patient, assume good intent, and critique ideas rather than
people. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated, in issues, pull
requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, email [jenish@scenr.io](mailto:jenish@scenr.io). Reports are read only by
the maintainers at Scenr Technologies and are kept confidential. You'll get a reply
within a few working days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, following the
Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
